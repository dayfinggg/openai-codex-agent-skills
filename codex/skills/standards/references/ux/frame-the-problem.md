# Frame the problem

- Identify the users, their context, the task they are trying to complete, the outcome they need, and the constraints around that task before proposing screens.
- Separate the user problem from a preferred feature or implementation. A request for a control is not evidence that the control solves the underlying problem.
- Write the riskiest assumptions and the evidence needed to accept or reject them.
- Design the smallest coherent journey that lets a user reach the outcome, including entry, interruption, failure, recovery, and completion.
- Consider the larger service around the screen, including messages, support, policies, devices, handoffs, and offline steps.
- Prioritize problems by user harm, frequency, business impact, and confidence in the evidence, not by stakeholder volume.
