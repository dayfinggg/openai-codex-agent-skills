# Delegate

Delegate an outcome, not a vague topic or a copy of the whole parent task.

## Confirm fit

Dispatch only with existing delegation authorization and available collaboration tools. This skill grants no additional authority. Honor a request to work without subagents and do not reconfirm existing permission. Choose a subtask with sufficient context, a clear completion condition, and no need for ongoing parent judgment. Keep shared-state ownership and integration with the primary agent.

## Write the brief

State the objective, why it matters, exact scope, inputs, relevant paths or sources, constraints, allowed side effects, expected artifact or answer, required evidence, and stopping condition. Include exclusions that prevent overlap with the parent or another agent.

For material delays, identify known dependency owners, an escalation condition, and the integration checkpoint. Treat estimates as uncertain rather than parent-level commitments.

Provide raw evidence rather than the intended conclusion when the subagent is evaluating or reviewing. Do not preload a suspected answer that would bias an independent result.

## Integrate

When execution is requested and authorized, send the brief through the collaboration tools and collect the result. Inspect artifacts and verify claims affecting the parent outcome. A status message alone does not prove completion.

## Output

For a brief-only request, return the brief without dispatch. If execution is requested but delegation is unavailable or prohibited, perform the work locally without inventing a delegation mechanism.
