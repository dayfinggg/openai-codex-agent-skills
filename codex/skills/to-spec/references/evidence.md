# Evidence for Specifications

Use this reference when assessing requirement quality, traceability, or acceptance criteria.

## Applied principles

1. State requirements as necessary, singular, unambiguous, feasible, and verifiable behavior or constraints.
2. Preserve traceability from desired outcome through requirements to acceptance evidence.
3. Separate requirements from design choices unless a design decision is already settled and constraining.
4. Record explicit scope boundaries and unresolved decisions rather than turning them into assumptions.

ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148 defines requirements-engineering processes and guidance for requirements information items:

- [ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018](https://www.iso.org/standard/72089.html)

The standard is broader than a document template. Apply its quality principles without importing unnecessary ceremony into a small specification.

Prototypes can reduce ambiguity and support validation when text cannot settle a material interaction or feasibility question:

- [An empirically based model of software prototyping](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10664-023-10331-w)
