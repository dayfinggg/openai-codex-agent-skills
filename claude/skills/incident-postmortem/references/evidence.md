# Evidence for Postmortems

Use this reference when reconstructing causes or defining corrective actions.

## Applied principles

1. Use a blameless account grounded in what people knew, the system conditions, and the safeguards available at the time.
2. Separate impact, trigger, proximate cause, contributing conditions, detection, response, and recovery.
3. Prefer a small set of corrective actions with measurable completion evidence.
4. Treat the postmortem as incomplete if actions are not followed through.

Google SRE guidance defines a useful postmortem around impact, timeline, root cause and triggers, contributing factors, what worked, what failed, and action items:

- [Postmortem Culture: Learning from Failure](https://sre.google/sre-book/postmortem-culture/)

The SRE Workbook emphasizes that actions need owners and verifiable end states, and that producing a document without follow-through does not reduce recurrence:

- [Postmortem Culture in the SRE Workbook](https://sre.google/workbook/postmortem-culture/)

Do not turn a complex system failure into a single human-error label. Accountability comes from accurate evidence and completion of corrective work.
